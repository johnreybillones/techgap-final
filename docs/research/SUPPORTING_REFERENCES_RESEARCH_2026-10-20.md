## Keep three different relationship meanings separate

1. **Taxonomy relation:** one concept is broader, narrower or associated with another. This supports terminology and retrieval, as documented in the [SKOS Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/).
2. **Learning prerequisite:** a provider explicitly recommends or requires prior knowledge for particular learning material. The [MDN DOM lesson](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Core/Scripting/DOM_scripting) supplies a concrete example; its scope must remain attached to that lesson.
3. **Coverage implication:** particular syllabus evidence supports a particular job competency under an explicit assessment rule. This is a TechGap analytical decision. Neither of the first two relations establishes it automatically. A technical definition such as [TypeScript's documented relationship to JavaScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/docs/handbook/typescript-in-5-minutes.html) can help assess a claim, but cannot replace missing course evidence.

Therefore a missing syllabus outcome remains missing even when an external source explains the technology well. A learning prerequisite can add a preparatory roadmap step without receiving current-coursework score credit. This preserves the separate roles of academic evidence, job evidence and supporting knowledge.

## Consequences for the planned flow

- **T3:** reference-assisted normalization is feasible to investigate; preserve source-backed unmatched terms rather than forcing catalog matches. ESCO's actual usefulness for this collection remains unmeasured.
- **T5:** retain logical coverage as conditional on explicitly supported, evaluated directional rules and sufficient syllabus evidence. The sources above do not complete that rule set.
- **T8:** reference material can support proposed content and explanations; a target course must still be justified by syllabus evidence. References cannot establish official track applicability, curriculum placement, allocated hours or institutional constraints absent from the JSON.
- **T10:** provider pages can supply resource destinations and limited preparation guidance. Resource coverage, suitability, ordering beyond the stated prerequisites and link maintenance remain unresolved. These two examples are not a collected resource catalog.

If references are later adopted, each used claim needs its exact source/passage, concept or resource scope, provider/version or retrieval date, relationship type/direction, and TechGap evaluation basis. This is proposed traceability information, not extracted syllabus or JobStreet metadata. Official authorship improves provenance; it does not guarantee suitability, complete coverage, correct linking or accurate downstream results. JobStreet posting counts remain the demand evidence, syllabus JSON remains the academic evidence, and extraction repairs remain necessary before declaring an analysis complete.
